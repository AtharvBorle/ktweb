EDUCATIONNamo Vidyanidhi - Scholarships for Meritorious Students

In response to financial constraints faced by underprivileged children aspiring for education, the "Namo Vidyanidhi" initiative offers scholarships of ₹10,000 each to 10,000 meritorious students. These scholarships are paid directly to the respective schools, ensuring financial assistance reaches those who need it most. To date, scholarships have been distributed to 1000 student beneficiaries.

HEALTHSetting up Janaushadhi Kendras

Janaushadhi Kendra generic medicine stores provide medicines at almost 70-90% reduced rates, helping citizens save big on their medical requirements. In Bengaluru South, Tejasvi Surya has made it a mission to set up a maximum number of PMBJKs and alleviate the medical expenditure of the poor. While in 2019, there were 14 Janaushadhi Kendras in Bengaluru South Constituency, over the course of 3 years, the number of kendras has increased exponentially to 112 making Bengaluru the city with the highest number of these generic medicine stores in the country.

Infrastructure - Transport & MobilityPradhan Mantri Awas Yojana

Tejasvi Surya reviews the scheme aimed at achieving housing for all and making affordable housing accessible to low and moderate-income citizens across the nation. In Bengaluru South, 1,90,646 houses have been built for the urban poor with a total investment of ₹3,239 crore.

LabourSouth India's 1st Resting Point for Delivery Partners:

Tejasvi Surya set up South India's 1st Resting Point for Delivery Partners at Vega City Mall in association with Zomato & Vega City. With essential amenities such as first-aid, drinking water, & clean restrooms, the point will serve as a place for all delivery partners to rest, rejuvenate, and recharge.

SportsBengaluru’s biggest Sports Festival

MP Sports Utsava was held on 17th and 18th December 2022 where 5000+ players competed in badminton, football, and table tennis across 42 avenues with India's football legend Shri Sunil Chhetri as Chief Guest.

EXPLORE VARIOUS INITIATIVES BY TEJASVI

Tejasvi has introduced a range of schemes, catering to diverse needs in education, healthcare and infrastructure. With an uncanny ability to foresee people’s problems, he implements technology-driven solutions to foster progress and efficiency.

NaMo Dialysis Centre

Tejasvi Surya set up the country’s first dialysis centre with no billing counter at the KSRTC Hospital, Jayanagar. The haemodialysis procedures being done here will be completely free. Established under MPLADS Funds, the centre is a Game changer for the poor with savings up to Rs 3000 per session.

Bharatiya Janata Yuva Morcha

Tejasvi Surya is the Youngest and First Kannadiga to be the President of the Youth Wing of the Largest Political Party of the world. BJYM is the strongest voice of India’s youth with the vision of grooming next generation leaders in the fields of governance and administration.
