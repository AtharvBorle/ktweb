KEY Issues
Kasba Peth, Pune Infrastructure

Issue: Congested City

Urban Mobility

Kunal is actively advancing key infrastructure projects in Kasba Peth, Pune. He played a pivotal role in expediting approvals for the long-pending Suburban Rail project, which had been delayed for over 40 years.

Moreover, he's been instrumental in securing approvals for the Kasba Peth, Pune Metro's Phase 2A and 2B, set to benefit approximately 7.5 lakh riders and reach completion by 2025.

Kunal Tilak's efforts also extend to fast-tracking Central Government approval for Phase 3 of the metro, which will traverse Kasba Peth, Pune's Outer Ring Road. These actions underscore his commitment to enhancing Kasba Peth, Pune's critical infrastructure and addressing longstanding transportation challenges.

Education

Issue: Lack of access to quality education

NaMo Vidyanidhi
Children of Kasba Peth, Pune fare poorly in the SSLC exams. Children dropping out at Class 10 do not return to the system again. To tackle this, Kunal introduced a scheme that guarantees access to quality education and provides incentives for good performance.

In response to financial constraints faced by underprivileged children aspiring for education, the ‘Namo Vidyanidhi’ initiative offers scholarships of ₹10,000 each to 10,000 meritorious students in Kasba Peth, Pune. These scholarships are paid directly to the respective schools, ensuring financial assistance reaches those who need it most.

Join this movement driven by citizens to help children in need and to provide them with access to education leading to social transformation.

Healthcare

Issue: Unaffordability of quality medicines

Highest Jan Aushadhi Kendras in the country

Janaushadhi Kendras provide generic medicines at almost 70-90% reduced rates, helping citizens save big on their medical requirements. In Kasba Peth, Pune, Kunal has made it a mission to set up a maximum number of Janaushadhi Kendras to ensure access to quality medicines and alleviate the medical expenditure of the poor.

While in 2019, there were 14 Janaushadhi Kendras in Kasba Peth, Pune Constituency, over the course of 4 years, the number of kendras has increased exponentially to 112.

Kasba Peth, Pune has the highest number of these generic medicine stores in the country benefitting over 2 lakh people saving 7 crores in Kasba Peth, Pune.

Meet Kunal

Kunal Tilak is a dedicated grassroots worker who has advanced in his career through unwavering commitment and hard work

Kunal Tilak represents the vibrant constituency ofKasba Peth, Pune. Here, the juxtaposition ofHeritage and Modern Progress, alongsideFilter Coffee and Filter Array JavaScript, epitomizes the unique blend of tradition and innovation.

He embodies the values of civilizational causes and theSangathan ethos while representing the aspirations of New India. His journey underscores his deep commitment to grassroots efforts, allegiance to the BJP's principles, and dedication to serving India's future.

Best Debutant Parliamentarian of the Year

As a dedicated member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), Kunal has garnered significant recognition in Indian politics as of 2022. Notably, he was awarded the 'Best Debutant Parliamentarian of the Year' by Lokmat Group for his political acumen and maturity in Parliament.



In the 17th Legislative Assembly, he actively participated in 32 debates on issues of national and public importance and raised a total of 347 questions, underscoring his commitment to addressing crucial matters through legislative means.

First Leader & Youngest National President of the BJP's youth wing, the Bharatiya Janata Yuva Morcha (BJYM)

As the first Leader to hold this position, Kunal Tilak has made history. During his tenure, he prioritised the development of a training module for party workers, aiming to equip them with the skills and knowledge required to contribute effectively to the party's goals. His accomplishments reflect his dedication to his political career and his commitment to serving the nation's interests, particularly in parliamentary work and youth engagement initiatives.

Kunal Tilak is a lawyer by profession and the founding partner of Trust Law, with offices in Kasba Peth, Pune, Delhi & Chennai. He has provided legal representation and secured justice for numerous pro-India voices who had been unfairly targeted by the Congress State Government.

EXPLORE VARIOUS INITIATIVES BY KUNAL TILAK

Kunal has introduced a range of schemes, catering to diverse needs in education, healthcare and infrastructure. With an uncanny ability to foresee people’s problems, he implements technology-driven solutions to foster progress and efficiency.

NaMo Dialysis Centre

Kunal Tilak set up the country’s first dialysis centre with no billing counter at the KSRTC Hospital, Jayanagar. The haemodialysis procedures being done here will be completely free. Established under Constituency Development Fund Funds, the centre is a Game changer for the poor with savings up to Rs 3000 per session.
