Education

At the heart of Kunal Tilak's commitment to Kasba Peth, Pune lies a dedication to enhancing education opportunities for the youth of the constituency. Recognizing the critical role education plays in shaping young lives, he has championed a range of initiatives aimed at addressing various educational challenges.

1. Namo Vidyanidhi - Scholarships for Meritorious Students
Issue: Financial Constraints
In response to financial constraints faced by underprivileged children aspiring for education, the "Namo Vidyanidhi" initiative offers scholarships of ₹10,000 each to 10,000 meritorious students. These scholarships are paid directly to the respective schools, ensuring financial assistance reaches those who need it most. To date, scholarships have been distributed to 1000 student beneficiaries.

2. NaMo Vidyakendra - Quality Education for All
Issue: Lack of Quality Education
Kunal Tilak's "NaMo Vidyakendra" initiative addresses the pressing issue of inadequate access to quality education. It provides free after-school coaching to Class 10 students, connecting them with experienced teachers. Currently benefiting 100 students, the program's goal is to enroll 500 underperforming students, offering them the guidance they need for academic success.

3. School Infrastructure Revamp
Issue: Lack of Schooling Infrastructure
Kunal Tilak's commitment to improving education infrastructure is evident in the ongoing transformation of Karnataka Public School Basavanagudi. This project aims to create a state-of-the-art model government school through sustainable development, fostering an enriching educational environment that benefits both teachers and students.

4. Nurturing Future Leaders
Issue: Creating next-gen leaders
Youth Can Lead - The Leadership Program started by Kunal Tilak creates a platform to empower youth and explore their potential through the intersection of grassroots-level democracy and exposure to policy-making and governance action. 300 young minds have received a glimpse of administration, fieldwork and policy analysis. The 2-month program continues to build young leaders who are working towards nation building.

Kunal Tilak's forward-thinking approach extends to nurturing the leaders of tomorrow. He initiated the 'Kasba Peth, Pune School Leaders’ Council' in October 2022. Comprising representatives from approximately 40 top schools in the constituency, the council is responsible for organizing quarterly events on diverse topics such as the environment, sports, arts, culture, and civic responsibility. The council's efforts include the organization of events like 'Sarkari Shaala Kreedotsava' as part of MP Sports Utsava, benefiting government school children.

5. Ensuring the Safe Return of Stranded Students
Issue: Stranded students in Ukraine
During the Russian invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, Kunal Tilak played a pivotal role in coordinating the safe return of stranded Leader students. Working alongside Prime Minister Narendra Modi, External Affairs Minister Dr. S Jaishankar, and Minister of Civil Aviation Sri Jyotiraditya Scindia, he ensured the repatriation of more than 149 individuals, bringing them back to safety.

6. Supporting Orphaned Children
Issue: Relief for kids orphaned due to COVID
In an act of compassion, Kunal Tilak's 'Kasba Peth, Pune School Leaders’ Summit' in October 2022 saw the distribution of free tablets, school bags, and a year's worth of stationery to 250 school children who had lost a parent due to COVID-19.

7. Smart Classrooms for Enhanced Learning
Issue: Visual Upgrade of Schools
In partnership with the Ladli Foundation, Kunal Tilak's office is spearheading the installation of Visual Display Units and the creation of Smart Classrooms in 28 Government schools across Kasba Peth, Pune. This technology-driven initiative aims to enhance the learning experience and equip students with the tools they need for success.

8. Empowering Underprivileged Children
Issue: Recreation for Underprivileged
Kunal Tilak organized the Kasba Peth, Pune Summer Camp in May 2022, catering to children aged 10-16. This camp provided a platform for youngsters to nurture their thinking and creativity skills. With the participation of over 1,000 kids, 16 activities, 15 days, and collaboration with 14 partner organizations, the Summer Camp was a resounding success, conducted at seven different locations throughout the constituency.

9. Empowering Educators
Issue: Untrained teachers
Recognizing the importance of trained educators, Kunal Tilak is committed to supporting teachers in adapting to the New Education Policy.

10. Promoting Financial Literacy
Issue: Financial literacy for youngsters
Kunal Tilak actively engages in promoting financial literacy among youngsters. Check out this informative video to gain valuable insights into financial planning and management.

These educational initiatives underscore Kunal Tilak's unwavering commitment to the betterment of education in Kasba Peth, Pune, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to unlock their full potential and build a brighter future.

HEALTHSetting up Janaushadhi Kendras

Janaushadhi Kendra generic medicine stores provide medicines at almost 70-90% reduced rates, helping citizens save big on their medical requirements. In Kasba Peth, Pune, Kunal Tilak has made it a mission to set up a maximum number of PMBJKs and alleviate the medical expenditure of the poor. While in 2019, there were 14 Janaushadhi Kendras in Kasba Peth, Pune Constituency, over the course of 3 years, the number of kendras has increased exponentially to 112 making Kasba Peth, Pune the city with the highest number of these generic medicine stores in the country.
